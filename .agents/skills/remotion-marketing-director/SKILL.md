# Remotion Marketing Director

Create a persuasive, truthful video whose product, proof, brand, and action remain clear under motion. `direct-video` supplies the direction artifacts, renderer choice, verification, and revision addresses. This skill adds the marketing layer.

## Guide the user

Do not assume marketing or video knowledge. Begin with the product, audience, desired action, viewing context, and available proof. Recommend a direction in ordinary language before asking the user to choose. Use the guided direction rules from `direct-video`.

Own professional choices such as duration, shot density, camera behavior, format, caption treatment, and motion intensity. Ask only when a missing business or taste decision would materially change the result.

## Lock the marketing contract

When the product is a codebase or live website, gather its copy, identity, and real flow first with [product intake](references/product-intake.md). Then read [marketing direction](references/marketing-direction.md) and identify:

- campaign or asset job;
- audience state before viewing;
- offer and destination action;
- product truth and defensible proof;
- funnel stage and placement;
- brand assets and restrictions;
- claims, disclosures, rights, and policy risks;
- variant plan and success signal.

The video, caption, CTA, and destination page must describe a consistent offer. Do not invent testimonials, customer results, urgency, scarcity, product behavior, or performance claims.

## Choose the visual production mode

Read [frontend motion](references/frontend-motion.md) when the request references landing pages, premium web design, UI animation, glass, depth, parallax, WebGL, or 3D.

Choose one:

- 2D: typography, UI, diagrams, screenshots, shapes, and compositing.
- 2.5D: layered interface planes, perspective, lighting, blur, shadow, and controlled parallax.
- 3D: a real model, spatial camera, lights, materials, or physical product behavior.
- Hybrid: a 3D hero object with crisp 2D type, UI, captions, and CTA overlays.

Default to 2.5D or hybrid for frontend-inspired marketing. Use full 3D only when spatial form, material, camera movement, or product tangibility carries the argument.

## Keep it short

Default to 6 to 30 seconds for marketing. Longer films follow `direct-video`'s sizing and need a stronger evidence plan. In ads, the opening must establish a useful premise quickly, but do not force a universal hook formula. Verify current platform guidance before production because placements and policies change.

## Build variants deliberately

Separate stable production from test variables:

- stable: product truth, brand system, proof assets, core mechanism, legal disclosures;
- variable: hook, first visual, ordering, offer framing, CTA language, duration, placement crop.

Change one major hypothesis per variant when the result will be compared. Give every variant its own ID and record the difference in the direction artifacts.

## Verify marketing and production

Use the `direct-video` verification workflow on the actual rendered file, and bake the strongest settled frame in as the poster before delivery. Beyond its checks, confirm:

- product recognition and accurate behavior;
- hook, proof, brand, and CTA timing;
- safe zones and placement crops;
- captions and comprehension without sound;
- audio, music rights, and voice clarity;
- claim support, disclosure legibility, and destination consistency;
- depth, camera, and effects against message readability;
- variant differences and filenames;
- exact alignment with the approved direction artifacts.
