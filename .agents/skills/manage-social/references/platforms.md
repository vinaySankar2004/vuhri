# Platforms

Checked 2026-10-20. Platforms change these often, so recheck a figure before relying on it and
update its date here. "Official" means the platform's own page; anything else is marked.

## Instagram

- **Reels:** 9:16, 1080x1920 or larger, at least 30 fps, H.264 and AAC. Only Reels of 3 minutes
  or less can be recommended ([official](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)).
- **Safe zone:** Meta publishes one for ads only, so use it for everything: keep text out of
  the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% at each side. On 1080x1920 that leaves about 950x979 px
  ([official](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels)).
- **Grid and covers:** the profile grid crops to 3:4, so centre the cover's subject.
- **Carousels:** up to 20 slides.
- **Ranking:** Instagram names watch time, likes and sends as the top signals, and suggests
  reading average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach
  ([Mosseri, January 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)).
  Original content replaces reposts in recommendations, now including photos and carousels.
  Trial Reels, shown first to non-followers, need 1,000 followers.
- **Engagement bait:** asking for a specific word, number or emoji can keep a post out of
  recommendations ([report](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-single-word-ctas-longer-reels/718151/)).
  Ask a real question instead, such as "which would you pick, and why?"
- **Alt text:** Reels have no alt text field, and the caption stands in for it. Burn short
  captions into the video and write a descriptive post caption.

## X

- **Video:** aspect ratio between 1:3 and 3:1, up to 60 fps, H.264 High Profile and AAC.
  Portrait 720x1280 is the recommended size, and 1080p is for subscribers only
  ([official](https://docs.x.com/x-api/media/quickstart/best-practices)). Test the first upload.
- **Images:** 5 MB each, alt text up to 1,000 characters. Videos take no alt text.
- **Ranking:** the open-sourced ranker scores predicted replies, reposts, quotes, dwell time,
  video watching, profile clicks and follows. Mutes, blocks and "not interested" count
  against a post. New authors get a boost, and repeated posts from one author are damped
  ([official code](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm)).
- **Automation:** automatic replies to people who engage are allowed. Automated posting on
  trends and spam are not.

## YouTube Shorts

- Up to 3 minutes, vertical or square. A Short over one minute with any active copyright claim
  is blocked ([official](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)).
- Repetitive, mass-produced content is excluded from monetisation, so vary templates.
- Studio reports how many viewers stayed versus swiped away.

## Across platforms

- **Music:** audio rendered into a video is not covered by any app's in-app music licence.
  Use original audio, or a licence that names every platform the video goes to.
- **Results and claims:** back every performance claim, show losses as visibly as wins, and
  label simulated results as simulated. Keep a short disclosure on screen for the whole
  video and on every slide, not only in the caption or bio. The FTC acted against an
  investment-tips firm over unsupported earnings claims
  ([official](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/01/ftc-suit-requires-investment-advice-company-wealthpress-pay-17-million-deceiving-consumers)).
- **Third-party brands:** game footage, card art and logos in commercial posts go beyond most
  fan-content policies. Draw generic stand-ins, and use names and statistics only as facts.

## Cadence for a new account

These starting points are inferred from [Buffer's analysis of 2M posts](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
and its [growth playbook](https://buffer.com/resources/creator-growth-playbook).
Posting consistently across weeks mattered more than volume, and replying to comments lifted
engagement.

- Instagram: three Reels and one carousel a week. Reels reach further, and carousels draw more
  engagement.
- X: one or two posts a day, mostly text with one chart or image, and replies every day.
- YouTube Shorts: cross-post Reels once their audio is cleared for YouTube.
- Review at week four: watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach.
