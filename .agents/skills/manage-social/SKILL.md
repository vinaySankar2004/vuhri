# Manage social

Act as the person's social media manager. Each brand gets one space under `local/spaces/`,
which holds the account plan, the post log and any study notes. This skill holds the method.

## Plan the account

Write the plan into the space's `CONTEXT.md` and keep it current:

- who the account is for, and what a follower gets from it;
- two to four **series**: repeatable formats, each with a name, a job, a length and an ending
  (for example an explainer, a prediction, a receipt, a product tour);
- each platform's role, and a cadence the person can keep up for a month;
- the brand rules, linked rather than restated, and what is never posted.

Start smaller than feels ambitious. Two series posted on schedule teach more than five that
stall. Propose the plan, get a yes, then record it.

## Make a post

1. Name the series and the one thing the post should leave behind.
2. Build the asset:
   - **Video** goes through `direct-video` with the marketing or learning adapter. Vertical
     pieces follow its [short-form reference](../direct-video/references/short-form.md).
   - **Graphics and carousels** are one HTML page per frame in the brand's tokens, rendered to
     PNG at the platform size and inspected like any other artifact.
   - Use real data, real product renders and real results only.
3. Package it for each platform with the [platform reference](references/platforms.md):
   caption, alt text, cover frame, link, and any disclosure. Put everything for a post in its
   project's `final/`, one folder per platform.
4. Check before handover: every claim traces to a source, a result carries its window and a
   plain disclosure, rights are clear for music, names and images, the content sits inside
   the safe zones, and the post works with the sound off.

## Publish

By default the person posts. Hand over the files and a ready-to-paste caption for each
platform. If they ask the agent to post, they sign in themselves, and every single post needs
their yes before it goes out. Never reply, follow or send messages from their accounts
unprompted.

## Learn

- Log every post in the space's `posts.md`: date, platform, series, hook, length and link,
  then the numbers at about 48 hours and again at 7 days. Record views, watch time where the
  platform shows it, likes, comments, shares or sends, saves and follows.
- About monthly, or every ten posts, compare series and hooks. Record a lesson only when it
  shows up more than once, and mark it a candidate. Video-craft lessons go back into
  `direct-video` through its learning loop.
- One post never proves a cause.

## Study other creators

Record properties, not content: structure, pacing, endings and layout, with numbers and dates,
in the space's `research/`. Downloading someone's posts needs the person's yes; keep the files
inside the space and delete them once the notes are written. Never copy a creator's scripts,
recognisable sequences or visual identity.
